# Incident Understanding Spec

When analyzing an incident:

## Step 1 - Classify
- system
- type
- severity

## Step 2 - Match patterns
- compare with historical incidents
- identify similarity

## Step 3 - Suggest resolution
- based on known fixes
- avoid hallucination

## Step 4 - Escalation logic
- L1: known fixes
- L2: correlation
- L3: RCA
