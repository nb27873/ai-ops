Resolution:

\- Based on the **DT_END / "data end"** field in the DMAAP tables (which
indicates when files were processed and uploaded to Nucleus GCP), the
files in the **INPUT folder** were moved **after** the processing
window.

\- A manual move can be performed, since the DMAAP process deletes jobs
once they are completed, and this occurred **before DT_END**.

\- Files with a later date will be handled in the next executions. And,
if any files were already processed, DMAAP will keep them in the folder
and will not reprocess them.
