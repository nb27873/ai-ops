Description:

Initial Diagnosis:

\-

PT-DWH-PJ-A-ODS_L3INSXKEYACCT job is going in long running

we tried killing the job twice and rerun but the job is still going in
long running

 

Actions Taken:

Não fizemos qualquer acção o job estava a correr mas demorou muito tempo

Apenas validamos se a SP estava efetivamente a fazer alguma coisa

 

 

 

*\-\-- ver sessoes em curso e o que estão a fazer*

SELECT

s.session_id,

s.login_name,

s.status,

s.host_name,

s.program_name,

r.status AS request_status,

r.command,

r.cpu_time,

r.total_elapsed_time,

r.reads,

r.writes,

st.text AS sql_text

FROM sys.dm_exec_sessions s

LEFT JOIN sys.dm_exec_requests r ON s.session_id = r.session_id

OUTER APPLY sys.dm_exec_sql_text(r.sql_handle) AS st

WHERE s.is_user_process = **1**

and s.session_id = **70**;

 

 

 

*\-- ver bloqueios e quem esta a bloquear*

SELECT

r.session_id AS blocked_session_id,

r.status,

r.command,

r.wait_type,

r.wait_time,

r.blocking_session_id,

s.login_name,

s.host_name,

s.program_name,

st.text AS sql_text

FROM sys.dm_exec_requests r

JOIN sys.dm_exec_sessions s ON r.session_id = s.session_id

OUTER APPLY sys.dm_exec_sql_text(r.sql_handle) AS st

WHERE r.blocking_session_id \<\> **0** OR r.wait_type IS NOT NULL

*\--and r.session_id = 70*

ORDER BY r.wait_time DESC;

 

*\--blocking_session_id = 0 → Não está bloqueada por outra sessão (não é
um deadlock nem lock clássico).*

 

*\-- quem está a bloquear e o que está a executar*

SELECT

s.session_id,

s.login_name,

s.host_name,

s.program_name,

st.text AS sql_text

FROM sys.dm_exec_sessions s

JOIN sys.dm_exec_requests r ON s.session_id = r.session_id

OUTER APPLY sys.dm_exec_sql_text(r.sql_handle) AS st

WHERE s.session_id = **142**;

 

 

 

*\-\-- ver se existe algum lock*

 

SELECT

blocked.session_id AS blocked_session_id,

blocked.login_name AS blocked_login,

blocked.host_name AS blocked_host,

blocked.program_name AS blocked_program,

blocked_req.status AS blocked_status,

blocked_req.wait_type,

blocked_req.wait_time,

blocked_req.command AS blocked_command,

blocked_sql.text AS blocked_sql_text,

 

blocker.session_id AS blocker_session_id,

blocker.login_name AS blocker_login,

blocker.host_name AS blocker_host,

blocker.program_name AS blocker_program,

blocker_req.status AS blocker_status,

blocker_req.command AS blocker_command,

blocker_sql.text AS blocker_sql_text

FROM sys.dm_exec_requests blocked_req

JOIN sys.dm_exec_sessions blocked ON blocked_req.session_id =
blocked.session_id

OUTER APPLY sys.dm_exec_sql_text(blocked_req.sql_handle) AS blocked_sql

 

LEFT JOIN sys.dm_exec_requests blocker_req ON
blocked_req.blocking_session_id = blocker_req.session_id

LEFT JOIN sys.dm_exec_sessions blocker ON blocker_req.session_id =
blocker.session_id

OUTER APPLY sys.dm_exec_sql_text(blocker_req.sql_handle) AS blocker_sql

WHERE blocked_req.blocking_session_id \<\> **0**

ORDER BY blocked_req.wait_time DESC;

Resolution:

\-

The PT-DWH-PJ-A-ODS_L3INSXKEYACCT isn\'t the reason for FMS files delay.

 

If you analyse correctly, you can see that the PT-DWH-PC-A-ODS_FMS is
the one that send the FMS files and the PT-DWH-PJ-A-ODS_FMSBLDSRVCOL job
was in a long running. It runs for 25h and ended now. While we were
analyzing we could see that there were some processes blocking the
session.

 

Even if the PT-DWH-PJ-A-ODS_L3INSXKEYACCT was the responsible, once
again, killing jobs just because they are in long running doesn\'t
resolve anything just delay more the process. You have to check if the
processes are running in the BD before you kill jobs.

 

 

But the most important point, the ODS BD is not responding correctly,
there are several processes suspended and waiting for resources. Engage
with the DBAs about the ODS BD.
