INC000099266256 (P2) - Multiple job abort due to TALEND
MJ_DMaaP_Main\_\*\* job connection issue

Date: 07-05-2026

Requester: AO

Description:

We have multiple job abort and long running jobs in yesterday and
today\'s date.

 

As Dmaap jobs are picking Talend MJ_Dmaap_Main\*\* jobs.

 

The jobs are failing as we connection to the TALEND MAIN job they are
picking the same talend jobs and after doing TP to PE we rerun the job
still job is failing.

 

DST team is already aware of the issue as yesterday few MAIN jobs were
there but today max Are impacted due to which controlm is impacted.

 

 

Diagnosis:

 

Error on control m DMAAP jobs: ORA-20000: No main jobs available
ORA-06512

 

- We analyzed the script and found that the query used to validate
  whether there are Main jobs available is the following:

 

> talenduser@pt402adr: /var/SP/data1/dmaap/runFeed

 

Talend BD, schema DMAAP

 

with param as (

  with the in_execution query. Many of them were GS_ORG and LT_ORG jobs,
  so we tested killing one of the Control-M jobs.

Resolution:

 

We sent an email outlining our analysis and realized that the previous
day Manuel Andrade had made changes to the user analytics2@vodafone.com
without anticipating this impact.

After he corrected it, we tested the connection to TAC again and it was
successfully established.
